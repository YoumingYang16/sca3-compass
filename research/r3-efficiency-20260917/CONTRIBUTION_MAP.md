## Why study this separately?

It tests a specific, falsifiable efficiency claim: a worst-direction spectral
envelope can be replaced by an independent-orientation reference while
retaining full finite-calibration validity, without assuming finite noise
variance or changing the partial-conjunction target. This requires coupling
the correct shape-diagonal/spectrum distribution and integrating the actual
scale-summary error law, not simply estimating kappa more accurately or
turning up a threshold. The cost is smaller learning folds and reference
simulation. Whether the cost is worth paying is a separate paired experiment.

The finite-law geometric moment calculation adds a mechanistic scale comparison:
at N4 it changes the known-shape positive absolute-moment threshold from12to16.
It does not prove the full unknown-shape reference's tail index or superiority.

Completed component evidence strengthens the independent-orientation research
question: main-minus-scale-only lower endpoints are positive in all10nonnull
M0scenes. Geometric main-minus-median-orientation extra gain is independently
supported only in t3N12, not universally. Thus do not present the geometric
summary alone as the main proven cause of useful improvement. The full2x2
means and architecture/training-size qualification are in R3_RESULTS.md.

## What cannot yet be advertised

Not the first sample-split projection, first finite-sample invariant detector,
new general e-calibrator, globally optimal/minimax method, arbitrary-dependence
gene test, drift-robust method, real SCA3 discovery, or top-tier publication.
Closest finite-sample adaptive CFAR work is a specific priority-check gap.
Even a successful C001 utility result alone cannot close that gap or user
criterion8. An honest paper can present a scoped construction/comparison;
claiming a novel general method would require stronger differentiation.
