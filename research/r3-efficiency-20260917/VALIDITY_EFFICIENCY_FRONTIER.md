## What an empirical Pareto statement may mean

At equal model-guarantee grade and observed information, a higher paired Power
with tolerable computational/calibration cost is an improvement of the observed
frontier for those scenes. A complete method need not be pointwise dominant.
R3 additionally changes the split allocation, so component/scene reversals must
be displayed. A same-grade winner in M0 does not dominate outside-M0 drift.

All methods use q=.05; actualFDR is not artificially adjusted to equal5% using
truth. The strong working model may attain more Power for narrower assumptions,
but no unproved statement converts its gap into a necessary guarantee price.
No fixed minimum positive gain was chosen; practical gains, intervals, extra
true discoveries, calibration requirements and cost are jointly evaluated.
