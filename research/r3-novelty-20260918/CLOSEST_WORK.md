# Closest work: what actually settles the audit

The decisive chain is **invariance -> exact Monte Carlo test ->
intersection-union PC -> calibrated evidence -> e-BH**. The full hypotheses
and theorem numbers are in R3_NOVELTY_THEOREM_MATRIX.md.

Five closest reference points, in order of role rather than prestige:

1. Dufour2006 Prop2.2: finite simulated ranks after nuisance elimination.
2. Wang–Owen Prop3.1: max-over-subset PC validity.
3. Vovk–Wang Prop2.1: converting valid p-values to evidence.
4. Wang–Ramdas Thm5.1/6.2: arbitrary-dependent evidence to finite FDR.
5. Lee–Ren Thm1: distinguishes their joint conditional boosting from R3's
   much simpler independent-PILOT selection. Its non-applicability is not
   evidence that R3 solved its missing conditional-law problem.

Recent e-PCH/e-Filter and SEFT sources were additionally checked, preventing
the claim that finite calibration/learning/PC/e-values are an untouched
combination. The potential upstream originality rests on the shape-reference
lemma, not on the final pipeline. Its rotation derivation is a direct
specialisation, with no demonstrated new general hypothesis or theorem.

The2010 CFAR paper is a relevant priority lead but its proof remains
unavailable, so it is not used as decisive evidence of exact equivalence.
Primary source links, version-qualified numbers and access limits are in the
matrix. No external algorithm package was installed or represented as run.
